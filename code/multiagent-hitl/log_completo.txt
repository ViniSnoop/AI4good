### 🔄 Rodada 3 de 20: A Arquitetura do Veto Soberano e o Risco de Ponto Único de Falha

**[Agent Alpha - The Architect (qwen2.5:7b)]**
> For the immediate deployment of the benevolent AI in healthcare and emergency response, establish a dual-layered oversight model involving both AI-driven decision support and direct human supervision. Implement phased rollouts to allow continuous monitoring and validation by an interdisciplinary council. Ensure all critical decisions have a human final veto point to address potential risks and maintain accountability, with ultimate control retained by the Comandante.

**[Agent Beta - The Adversary (llama3.1:8b)]**
> I've identified several logical vulnerabilities in this proposal: The dual-layered oversight model is redundant at best, as AI-driven decision support can perpetuate systemic biases if not properly addressed. Furthermore, relying on an interdisciplinary council for continuous monitoring may lead to groupthink and delayed reactions in high-pressure emergency situations. Most critically, the human final veto point creates a single-point-of-failure vulnerability, allowing a single individual (the Comandante) to override critical decisions without sufficient transparency or accountability.

**🏛️ [Conselho Consultivo de Arbitragem - Votação dos Modelos Locais]**
* **Juror 1 - Ministral-3 (3B) [ministral-3:3b]:**
  > **[VOTE: COMPROMISE]**
  > Phased deployment with layered oversight ensures modular, adaptable integration without overloading critical emergency systems. A human-in-the-loop veto safeguards accountability, while the interdisciplinary council enables real-time validation across diverse expertise. The Comandante's control serves as a high-level containment layer, allowing emergency recalibration if unforeseen risks emerge.
* **Juror 2 - Mistral (7B) [mistral:7b]:**
  > **[VOTE: ALPHA]**
  > The Alpha proposal prioritizes systemic coherence and governance by establishing a dual-layered oversight model combining AI decision support with direct human supervision. This approach ensures continuous monitoring, validation, and accountability through an interdisciplinary council. Maintaining a human final veto point directly retains ultimate control with the Comandante.
* **Juror 3 - Gemma-3 (4B) [gemma3:4b]:**
  > **[VOTE: COMPROMISE]**
  > While Alpha's layered oversight model is a valuable starting point for incorporating AI responsibly, Beta's critique regarding human accountability is paramount. A true compromise necessitates integrating Beta's concerns—safeguarding against unintended consequences and latency—alongside Alpha's structured approach to ensure ethical deployment in sensitive areas.

**[Síntese do Orquestrador - Agent Gamma]**
* **Evolução Estratégica:** A Rodada 3 consolida a transição para a ação: início do piloto em saúde e triagem de emergência sob governança dual (suporte algorítmico + comitê interdisciplinar), ancorando a soberania no veto do Comandante.
* **Placar do Conselho:** **2 votos [COMPROMISE], 1 voto [ALPHA], 0 votos [BETA]**. O conselho apoia a implementação imediata proposta por Alpha, mas exige mecanismos que impeçam latência e viés na triagem.
* **Ponto Sensível:** O dilema do *Ponto Único de Falha (Single Point of Failure)* apontado por Beta: ao concentrar o veto final absoluto na figura do Comandante, cria-se uma vulnerabilidade de sobrecarga cognitiva e falta de contrapesos institucionais em situações de emergência médica de alta velocidade.
